# Decision

Retain as a source-only candidate for more evidence. Do not promote or replace
the running three-rank image. The compact conceptual series replays exactly on
the pinned current heads, and the obsolete managed-weight adapter is removed.
The new B12X loader path, native ABI, output quality, memory, and serving speed
still require target-hardware qualification. A matching vLLM nightly image was
not available for the pinned commit at the time of this source audit. The
Dockerfile and source identity checks pass on dgx1; its native compilation is
deliberately deferred while all three inference ranks are running.
